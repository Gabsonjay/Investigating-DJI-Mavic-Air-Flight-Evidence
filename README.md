# Investigating-DJI-Mavic-Air-Flight-Evidence

<h1>Gabriel - Investigating-DJI-Mavic-Air-Flight-Evidence </h1>



<h2>Description</h2>
Investigators recovered an Android logical acquisition associated with the DJI GO 4 application and two representations of DJI Mavic Air internal microSD media. The enquiry is whether the sources support a reliable account of aircraft activity, recorded media, and the relationship between the mobile application and microSD evidence. The case material may contain flight locations, images, and records relating to people. This is an offline examination of authorised course artefacts. It does not authorise you to fly, power on, connect to, synchronise, reset, or change settings on any drone, mobile device, account, cloud service, or removable media...
